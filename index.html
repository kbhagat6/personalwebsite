About Me

I'm a software engineer with experience building production systems across cross-platform device management, AI-powered workflows, cloud infrastructure, and security. At Microsoft, I build device management software for Apple and Linux devices, with work spanning authentication, enrollment, service integrations, secure communications, and launch readiness for new OS releases. I've resolved incidents affecting tens of thousands of devices and built automation that became a standard across the organization. I also use AI to improve engineering workflows within my team and share successful approaches with other teams when they offer broader value.

I hold an M.S. in Computer Science, focused on Machine Learning, Computational Perception, and Robotics, and a B.S. in Computer Engineering, focused on Distributed Systems and Robotics, from Georgia Tech. I enjoy solving problems at the intersection of software engineering, AI, infrastructure, and security, particularly when complex workflows can be transformed into reliable, practical tools.
